Singapore visa for Indians

Indians need a visa for Singapore. Applications are filed only through authorised agents like us (or a Singapore citizen sponsor), and the e-Visa usually arrives within a week.

Visa typeTourist e-Visa
StayUsually up to 30 days
Government fee₹2,100
Processing3–5 working days
Good to know: The 96-hour transit facility applies only if you are flying on to a third country and hold a visa from Australia, Canada, Germany, Japan, New Zealand, Switzerland, the UK or the USA valid for at least a month.

Processing time: Usually 3–5 working days, excluding weekends. Apply within 30 days of arrival.

Documents checklist

  • Form 14A, completed and signed
  • Passport valid at least 6 months from entry
  • Recent colour photo, 35×45 mm, white background
  • Confirmed return tickets and hotel booking
  • 3-month bank statement, ITR or salary slips
  • Cover letter for the trip

How we apply for you

  1. Send your passport front and back pages and travel dates on WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm the right visa and send a personalised checklist.
  3. We fill the application and review every document before submission.
  4. We track the application and send you the decision as soon as it is issued.

Our service fee is listed on the Pricing page. Government fees are charged at actuals and are not refundable if a visa is refused.

Before you fly

  • Fill the SG Arrival Card within 3 days before arrival, on the ICA website or MyICA app. It is free.
  • A new passport cancels the existing visa.
  • A visa does not guarantee entry; the officer at the checkpoint decides.
