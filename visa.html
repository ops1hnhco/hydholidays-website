Visa services

Country guides for ten of the most popular destinations for Indian travellers, plus end-to-end help with the application. The decision is always the embassy's; we make sure the file is complete.

Country guides

Top visa destinations for Indians

Our most-requested destinations, in order of how often Indians travel there (Ministry of Tourism data).

AE

UAE (Dubai)

30 or 60-day e-visa, usually in about 48 hours. Visa on arrival only with certain US/EU visas.

e-VisaUS

United States

B1/B2 visitor visa, valid up to 10 years. Interview required; slots in India are 6–12 months out.

InterviewTH

Thailand

30 days visa-free since 15 Sept 2026. Fill the TDAC arrival card within 72 hours of arrival.

Visa-freeSG

Singapore

e-Visa filed through an authorised agent, ready in 3–5 working days. SG Arrival Card required.

e-VisaGB

United Kingdom

Standard Visitor visa, £135 for 6 months. Decision usually within 3 weeks of biometrics.

VFS appointmentCA

Canada

Visitor visa (TRV), CAD 100 + CAD 85 biometrics. Processing from India about a month.

Online + biometricsMY

Malaysia

30 days visa-free until 31 Dec 2026. Submit the MDAC arrival card within 3 days of arrival.

Visa-freeAU

Australia

Visitor visa subclass 600, AUD 250. Most clean applications decided in 3–6 weeks.

OnlineVN

Vietnam

90-day e-visa, USD 25 single entry, issued in about 3 working days.

e-VisaEU

Schengen (Europe)

One visa for 29 European countries, €90. Frequent travellers can qualify for 2 and 5-year visas.
