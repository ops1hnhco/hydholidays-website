Vietnam visa for Indians

Indians need a visa for mainland Vietnam. The e-visa is applied for online, allows up to 90 days, and usually arrives in about 3 working days.

Visa typee-Visa
StayUp to 90 days
Government feeUSD 25 single / 50 multiple
ProcessingAbout 3 working days
Good to know: Phu Quoc island is visa-free only if you fly in and out of Phu Quoc directly and do not visit the mainland. Any mainland travel needs an e-visa.

Processing time: About 3 working days, up to a week in peak season. Apply 1–2 weeks before travel.

Documents checklist

  • Passport bio-page scan; passport valid 6+ months with 2 blank pages
  • Photo, 4×6 cm, white background, no glasses
  • Hotel name and address in Vietnam
  • Planned entry and exit airports
  • Travel dates

How we apply for you

  1. Send your passport front and back pages and travel dates on WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm the right visa and send a personalised checklist.
  3. We fill the application and review every document before submission.
  4. We track the application and send you the decision as soon as it is issued.

Our service fee is listed on the Pricing page. Government fees are charged at actuals and are not refundable if a visa is refused.

Before you fly

  • Use only the official evisa.gov.vn portal; many lookalike sites charge extra.
  • Your name and passport number must match the passport exactly, or you will need a new application.
  • The fee is not refunded if the e-visa is refused.
