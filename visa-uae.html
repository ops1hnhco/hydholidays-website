UAE (Dubai) visa for Indians

Indians need a visa for the UAE. Most travellers get a 30-day or 60-day tourist e-visa before flying, filed through a licensed sponsor such as us.

Visa typeTourist e-visa
Stay30 or 60 days
Government feeAbout AED 265 (30-day)
ProcessingAbout 48 hours
Good to know: Visa on arrival is only for Indians holding a valid US visa or Green Card, or an EU visa or residence permit, valid at least 6 months. Reports from July 2026 say UK-only visa holders are no longer eligible, and the status of Canada, Australia, Japan, Korea, Singapore and New Zealand holders is unclear. If you are relying on visa on arrival, we recommend getting a regular e-visa instead.

Other fees

  • Agent or airline sponsor charges are added to the government fee.
  • Express (about 1–3 days) processing costs extra.

Processing time: About 48 hours for a standard tourist visa. Apply at least a week before travel.

Documents checklist

  • Passport valid at least 6 months, scan of first and last pages
  • Passport-size colour photo on a white background
  • Confirmed return ticket
  • Hotel booking or host's address
  • Travel insurance valid in the UAE (a visa condition)
  • For the 5-year visa: 6-month bank statement showing about USD 4,000 and health insurance

How we apply for you

  1. Send your passport front and back pages and travel dates on WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm the right visa and send a personalised checklist.
  3. We fill the application and review every document before submission.
  4. We track the application and send you the decision as soon as it is issued.

Our service fee is listed on the Pricing page. Government fees are charged at actuals and are not refundable if a visa is refused.

Before you fly

  • Airlines check visa status at check-in; carry a printout of your e-visa.
  • A tourist visa does not allow work.
  • Overstaying is fined for each extra day.
  • The 60-day visa on arrival cannot be extended.
