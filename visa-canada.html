Canada visa for Indians

Indians need a Temporary Resident Visa (visitor visa) for Canada. You apply online, give biometrics at a VFS centre in India, then send your passport for stamping.

Visa typeVisitor visa (TRV)
ValidityUp to 10 years
Government feeCAD 100 + CAD 85
ProcessingAbout 29 days

Other fees

  • Biometrics CAD 85 per person, CAD 170 per family.
  • Biometrics are generally valid for 10 years.
  • VFS service and passport courier charges are extra.

Processing time: IRCC's estimate for applications from India was about 29 days in September 2026, down from 83 days in February. Times are updated weekly, and the clock effectively starts once biometrics are given.

Documents checklist

  • Passport valid at least 6 months beyond travel, plus old passports
  • IRCC application forms and family information
  • Digital photo
  • Bank statements (4–6 months) and ITRs
  • Employer letter, leave approval and salary slips, or business documents
  • Itinerary or invitation letter from your Canadian host with their status proof
  • Purpose-of-visit letter
  • Proof of ties to India

How we apply for you

  1. Send your passport front and back pages and travel dates on WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm the right visa and send a personalised checklist.
  3. We fill the application, book your appointment and review every document before submission.
  4. We track the application and send you the decision as soon as it is issued.

Our service fee is listed on the Pricing page. Government fees are charged at actuals and are not refundable if a visa is refused.

Before you fly

  • Indians are not eligible for the Canadian eTA.
  • Fees are not refunded if the visa is refused.
  • Parents and grandparents can apply for a Super Visa for longer stays; requirements differ.

Official sources
