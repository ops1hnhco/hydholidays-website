## 3. Campaigns

Network and Search Partners off, keywords in phrase and exact match, ads shown 8 am – 10 pm (when
someone can answer calls and WhatsApp), English.

**Location: Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, "Presence: people in or regularly in" these places**, with
+20% bids for Hyderabad and Secunderabad (closest to the office, most likely to buy).

**Bidding:** "Maximise clicks" with a maximum CPC cap for the first 3–4 weeks (caps below). Once the
account has about 20–30 tracked enquiries, switch each campaign to "Maximise conversions".

## 5. Tracking (do this first)

1. **Done (live since 26 October 2026):** the site's `app.js` sends `generate_lead` (enquiry form sent),
   `whatsapp_click` and `phone_click` to Google Analytics (G-GRLZLMJQ62).
2. Google Analytics → Admin → Events: mark `generate_lead` and `whatsapp_click` as **key events**
   (they appear about a day after the first ones happen).
3. Google Ads → Tools → Data manager: **link Google Analytics** and import those two as conversions.
   Set `generate_lead` as primary; `whatsapp_click` primary too (most customers will message).
4. **Remove the unused "Add to cart" conversion action.**
5. Turn on **call reporting** (Google forwarding number) on the call asset, so real calls longer than
   60 seconds count as conversions, and you can see whether people actually called.

## 6. Order of work

1. Add funds (prepaid, at least ₹10,000 so ads don't stop mid-week). Billing is done by you.
2. Set up the tracking above (the website part is already live).
3. Pause the two Smart campaigns.
4. Build the four Search campaigns (paused), review, then enable.
5. Week 1–4: every week, check the search terms report, add negative keywords, move budget from
   campaigns with no enquiries to those with enquiries.
6. After about 20–30 enquiries: switch bidding to "Maximise conversions".
7. Later: landing pages per destination (e.g. `kerala.html`) and visa pages for Japan and China;
   both lower cost per enquiry.
