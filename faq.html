Frequently asked questions

Booking, payment, visa and refund questions we hear most often.

How do I book a package?

Send us your dates, number of travellers and destination. We send a written quote with hotel names. Pay the advance to confirm, and we send vouchers once the balance is paid.

Are flights included in package prices?

Only where the package says "Flights". Otherwise we quote flights from Hyderabad separately, so you can see the fare and choose timings.

Can you guarantee my visa?

No one can. The embassy decides. We make sure your application is complete and well documented, which avoids most refusals caused by missing paperwork.

How do I pay?

UPI, cards, net banking or wallets through our payment gateway, or a bank transfer. You receive a GST invoice for every payment.

How long do refunds take?

Once a refund is approved, we initiate it within 7 working days to the original payment method. Banks usually take another 5–7 working days. Details are in our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Do you charge TCS on international packages?

Yes, as required by law. TCS is collected against your PAN and can be claimed when you file your income tax return.

Can I change my travel dates?

Usually, subject to hotel and airline rules. Change charges from suppliers are passed on at actuals, plus our change fee where listed.
