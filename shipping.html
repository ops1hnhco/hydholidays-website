Shipping & Service Delivery Policy

We sell travel services, not physical goods. This page explains how and when your bookings are delivered.

On this pageNo physical shippingWhat you receive and whenPassport courierIf you have not received your documents

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. No physical shipping

Hyderabad Holidays Co. does not sell or ship physical products. All our services are delivered electronically or in person at our office.

2. What you receive and when

ServiceDelivered asDelivered within
Flight ticketE-ticket by email and WhatsAppWithin 2 hours of payment, in working hours
Hotel bookingHotel voucher by email and WhatsAppWithin 24 hours of supplier confirmation
Holiday packageBooking confirmation, vouchers and day-wise itineraryConfirmation within 24 hours; final vouchers 3–7 days before departure
e-VisaVisa copy by email and WhatsAppAs soon as the authority issues it
Sticker visaPassport returned by the visa centreCollect at our office or courier on request
InvoiceGST invoice by emailWithin 24 hours of payment

3. Passport courier

Where a passport is returned by courier, we use a tracked service and share the tracking number. Courier charges are listed on your quote. We are not responsible for delays caused by the visa centre or courier company, but we will follow up on your behalf.

4. If you have not received your documents

Check your spam folder first, then contact us at +91 70750 40406 or contact@hydholidays.com. We will resend documents immediately.
