Taxes

GST is charged as applicable on tour packages and service fees, and is shown separately on your invoice. Tax Collected at Source (TCS) is collected on overseas tour packages as required by the Income Tax Act, 1961, and a PAN is needed for every traveller. TCS can be claimed against your income tax.

When payment is due

  • Holiday packages: an advance of 30% confirms the booking; the balance is due 21 days before departure.
  • Flights: full payment at the time of ticketing, as airlines do not hold fares.
  • Visa services: service fee and government fees in full before filing.

How you can pay

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets through our secure payment gateway, or bank transfer to our current account. We never ask for your card PIN, OTP or CVV over phone, email or WhatsApp.

Cancellations and refunds are covered in our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
