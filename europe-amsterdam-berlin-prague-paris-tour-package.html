Frequently asked questions

How much does a Amsterdam, Berlin, Prague & Paris trip from Hyderabad cost?

Are flights from Hyderabad included?

No. This is a land package: you book your own flights to Europe, and everything from arrival is arranged for you.

What is the best time to visit Europe?

April to October for long days and the full sightseeing programme. Winter departures cost less, with Christmas markets in December.

Do I need a visa for Europe?
