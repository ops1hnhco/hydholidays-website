Good to know

  • Departures: Starts on Saturdays from Madrid, through the 2026–27 season. Operated by Europamundo as "Essential Europe"; we confirm open dates and the final price with your quote.
  • Best time to visit: April to October for long days and the full sightseeing programme. Winter departures cost less, with Christmas markets in December.
  • Visa: Indians need a Schengen visa; one visa covers every country on this route. The visa is not included in this package. Apply at least 6–8 weeks before travel.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Spain to Amsterdam trip from Hyderabad cost?

This 14N/15D package starts at €2,413 per person on twin sharing. The final price depends on your dates and hotel category; we send a written quote before you pay.

Are flights from Hyderabad included?

No. This is a land package: you book your own flights to Europe, and everything from arrival is arranged for you.

What is the best time to visit Europe?

April to October for long days and the full sightseeing programme. Winter departures cost less, with Christmas markets in December.

Do I need a visa for Europe?

Indians need a Schengen visa; one visa covers every country on this route. The visa is not included in this package. Apply at least 6–8 weeks before travel.

Can I change the itinerary?

Yes. You can add or drop nights, upgrade hotels, or travel as a private family or group departure. Tell us your dates and we rebuild the plan.
