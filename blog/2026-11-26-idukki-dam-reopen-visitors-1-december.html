Idukki Dam to reopen to visitors from 1 December

Kerala's Electricity Minister has directed that Idukki Dam be opened to visitors from 1 December, and boating at Anayirankal Dam is to resume. Families from Hyderabad planning a Kerala holiday this winter should confirm the details locally before adding these stops.

26 November 2026 · Travel advisory

What has been announced

Electricity Minister Sunny Joseph has directed that Idukki Dam be opened to visitors from 1 December. He gave the direction at a review meeting in Thodupuzha on Monday, 23 November 2026, according to press reports, including a PTI report.

The reports say the decision followed repeated requests from tourists. As the dam was earlier closed to the public, the move is being described as a reopening. The reports do not say how long it was closed.

For travellers, the timing matters. 1 December is the start of the December holiday season, when many families from Hyderabad and Andhra Pradesh plan trips to Kerala.

Anayirankal boating and wider plans

Boating at Anayirankal Dam, which had been suspended earlier, is also to resume on the minister's directions. No restart date has been reported, so it is too early to treat it as running.

The Electricity and Tourism departments also plan joint efforts to expand tourism in Idukki. The reports mention:

  • Electricity Department outreach centres at Muttom and Vagamon
  • Faster work on the Thunder Circuit tourism project
  • The move being part of wider efforts to use the district's tourism potential

What is still unclear

The reports describe a ministerial direction. They do not say whether the opening has been formally notified, and we could not find an official notification from the Kerala government or KSEB.

The pages we read also do not give the following details:

  • Entry fees
  • Visiting hours
  • How visitors will be admitted
  • Any security rules

Planning your trip

Rules at dams and hydel tourism sites can change at short notice. Please confirm the latest position with the Kerala hydel tourism or KSEB offices before you travel, and avoid fixing a day around the dam or the boating until it is confirmed open.

If you are planning a Kerala holiday this season, we can build your itinerary and add these stops once they are confirmed.

What to do

  • Check with the Kerala hydel tourism or KSEB offices for fees, timings and entry rules before you travel.
  • Do not plan around Anayirankal boating until a restart date is confirmed.
  • Keep your Idukki plans flexible in case the reopening is delayed.
