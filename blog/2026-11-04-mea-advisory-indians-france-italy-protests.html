MEA asks Indians in France and Italy to follow advisories

On 3 November 2026 the MEA asked Indians in France and Italy, including students, to follow embassy and local advisories amid protests. If you have a Europe trip booked or planned, check your flights, hotels and transport before you travel.

4 November 2026 · Travel advisory

What the MEA said

On Tuesday, 3 November 2026, MEA spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal told the weekly media briefing in New Delhi that Indian nationals in France and Italy, including students, should follow the advisories issued by Indian embassies and local authorities. This is because of ongoing protests in Europe.

This is an advisory only. The sources we read do not say that Schengen visas, entry rules or flights to France or Italy have changed.

France: curfews and transport delays

The Indian Embassy in France issued a travel and safety advisory after protests over the rising cost of living turned into violent clashes. It asked Indians and travellers to stay alert, especially in crowded places or where demonstrations are taking place.

The embassy said the unrest has led to localised curfews. Road travel and public transport may be delayed or cut. It advised travellers to check with airlines or travel agencies for schedule changes.

The advisory was posted on X on 21 October 2026. It asked Indians to avoid large gatherings and follow instructions from the French authorities. It also listed emergency contacts:

  • Embassy of India, Paris: +33 6 48 34 00 63, cons.paris@mea.gov.in
  • Consulate General of India, Marseille: +33 7 43 16 25 59, cgo.marseille@mea.gov.in

Italy: clashes in several cities

The Indian Embassy in Rome has also issued an advisory. It asks Indians in Italy to exercise caution, avoid crowded areas and protest locations, and follow local news.

The advisory followed clashes in several Italian cities. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said stones were thrown at police in Milan and officers were injured. In Bologna, the railway station was closed as a precaution.

What it means for your trip

Many families from Hyderabad plan Europe trips for the winter holidays or to visit relatives. We have not confirmed how the situation looks today, or whether the curfews are still in force, so please check the latest advisory before you go. We cannot say how long the protests will last.

What to do

  • Read the latest advisory from the Indian Embassy in Paris or Rome before you travel.
  • Check your flight schedule with the airline and confirm your hotel and tour bookings.
  • Save the embassy emergency contacts on your phone and avoid crowds and protest sites.
