New aviation rules from 1 Jan 2027: clearer fare breakdowns

The Centre has notified the Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam, 2026, which replaces the Aircraft Rules, 1937 and applies from 1 January 2027. It asks for clearer fare breakdowns and lets the government direct airlines in extraordinary situations, so Hyderabad travellers booking winter trips should know what changes and what does not.

10 November 2026 · Flights & airports

What has been notified

The Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam, 2026 replaces the Aircraft Rules, 1937. It comes into force on 1 January 2027, as per the PIB factsheet posted on 4 November 2026 and a Business Standard report of 1 November 2026.

Until that date, nothing changes for bookings or travel. If you are booking now for a trip after 1 January, the new rules will apply to travel from that date onwards.

Business Standard reports that the broad regulatory framework remains largely similar. Many existing requirements, including those on airline tariffs, have been retained.

Clearer pricing for passengers

According to the PIB factsheet, airlines must set tariffs based on factors including operating costs and publish them digitally. Scheduled operators must submit their tariffs to the DGCA for monitoring.

Booking websites and tickets must also show the total amount payable, with a breakdown of fares, taxes, fees and other charges. Business Standard adds that the breakdown must include:

  • Base fare
  • Taxes and fees
  • Cancellation charges
  • Refund conditions

Government power to step in

In extraordinary situations, the Central Government may issue proportionate, time-bound directions in the public interest. India TV News links the fare provisions to Rule 192 and also reports on this power to intervene.

This is not a fare cap. Indian Eagle, a secondary source, reports that the rules do not set a permanent cap and that airlines still set fares in normal circumstances. It also says the rules do not define what counts as extraordinary or set a price trigger. The pages we read do not say when the government would act.

No source we read says fares will fall or be capped. The pages we read describe the rules as applying to scheduled Indian airlines. We have not confirmed how they apply to foreign carriers or international fares.

What it means for your booking

The pages we read do not explain refund or cancellation rights in detail. Before paying, check your airline's own fare conditions. Rules can change, so check the latest before you travel.

What to do

  • Treat today's bookings as unaffected, as the new rules apply only from 1 January 2027.
  • Before paying, read the full fare breakdown and the airline's cancellation and refund conditions.
  • Check the latest rules and your airline's terms before you travel.
