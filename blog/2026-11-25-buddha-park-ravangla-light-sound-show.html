Buddha Park in Ravangla starts daily evening light and sound show

A light and sound show on Lord Buddha's life and teachings began at Buddha Park (Tathagata Tsal) in Ravangla, South Sikkim, on 24 November 2026. Travellers from Hyderabad who stop in Ravangla can now spend an evening at the park, which was earlier a daytime visit.

25 November 2026 · Destinations

What has started at Buddha Park

A light and sound show on the life and teachings of Lord Buddha was launched at Tathagata Tsal, also called Buddha Park, in Ravangla, South Sikkim. The launch was on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, as reported by Northeast News on 25 November.

The show is screened daily from 6 pm. A second report, from the Hindi outlet Prabhasakshi, also says it is held regularly from 6 pm and describes it as an audiovisual presentation of the Buddha's life and teachings.

What the show offers

The show uses lighting, sound and visual effects. According to Northeast News, the park's 130-foot (40 m) Buddha statue is lit up as the story unfolds.

The announcement was made by Bishnu Sharma, Additional Political Secretary to the Chief Minister for Namchi district. He said the aim is to draw tourists to Ravangla in the evening and make them stay longer. This should also bring business to local hotels and other tourism businesses.

The park complex has landscaped eco-gardens, Cho Djo Lake, a meditation centre and a museum gallery on the stages of the Buddha's life. It sits against Himalayan peaks, near Rabong Monastery. So you can visit the park in daylight and return for the show in the evening.

What is not yet confirmed

The reports do not give a ticket price, the length of the show or the language. They also do not explain how the show is presented, such as whether it is projected on the statue, a wall or a screen. No end date has been announced.

The show is described as daily or regular, but we have not seen official confirmation of the days it runs. Please check with the park or Sikkim Tourism before you plan your day around it.

Planning an evening in Ravangla

Ravangla is at about 7,000 ft, as per Prabhasakshi. Winter evenings there are cold, so carry warm clothing for the show, especially if you have children or elders with you.

If Ravangla is on your Sikkim itinerary, you may want to plan the day so that you reach the park in good time before 6 pm. Staying the night in Ravangla also saves you a late drive back.

What to do

  • Confirm the show timing, days and ticket details with Buddha Park or Sikkim Tourism before you go.
  • If Ravangla is on your Sikkim trip, plan to be at the park before 6 pm and consider an overnight stay there.
  • Pack warm clothing for the cold evening at about 7,000 ft.
