China visa: Hyderabad applicants now apply at Embassy in New Delhi

From 26 October 2026, the Chinese visa centres in India stopped taking new applications, and the Chinese Embassy in New Delhi and the Consulate General in Mumbai now handle visas directly. For travellers from Hyderabad, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, this means an online review first, an in-person step at the Embassy in New Delhi, and payment only by demand draft.

31 October 2026 · Visa news

Where you apply now

The Chinese visa centres in New Delhi, Mumbai and Kolkata stopped taking new applications on 26 October 2026. The Embassy and the Mumbai consulate now accept visa applications themselves.

The office that handles your application depends on where you live. Telangana and Andhra Pradesh are not named in the Embassy's notice, so they fall under "all other areas", which go to the Embassy in New Delhi. Mumbai covers only Maharashtra and Karnataka.

These rules apply to mainland China. Hong Kong and Macao have their own rules.

  • Maharashtra and Karnataka: Consulate General in Mumbai
  • Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh and West Bengal: Embassy in New Delhi, for now
  • All other areas, including Telangana and Andhra Pradesh: Embassy in New Delhi

How the new process works

You can apply yourself or through a travel agency. If you use an agency, you must provide the agency's qualification documents and the agent's ID.

Regular processing takes 7 working days, counted from the day after you hand in your passport. Express processing takes 4 working days.

The Embassy visa section is at Gate No. 4, Chinese Embassy, Nyaya Marg, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi-110021. Applications are taken 9:00am to 12:00pm and visas collected 3:00pm to 5:30pm, Monday to Friday. Tel: 91-11-24675259.

  • Step 1: Fill in the form and upload documents at consular.mfa.gov.cn/VISA/. Nothing is paid at this stage.
  • Step 2: After the approval email, you have 30 days to submit your passport, form and documents in person. Fingerprints are taken there if needed.
  • Step 3: Pay the fee when you collect the visa.

Documents and fingerprints

Keep these ready before you start the online form:

Until 31 December 2026, applicants for short-term visas (stays of up to 180 days) do not need to give fingerprints. People under 14 or over 70 never need to. However, the notice also says multiple-entry applicants should, in principle, give fingerprints in person, so multiple-entry and long-stay applicants should check before travelling to Delhi.

  • Passport valid for more than 6 months, with at least 2 blank visa pages
  • Copy of the passport information page, plus any earlier passport issued in the last 5 years
  • Two colour photos, 48mm × 33mm, white background, taken in the last 6 months (a 35×45mm Schengen-size photo will not do)
  • Tourist (L) visa only: 6 months of savings account statements with a steady average monthly balance of at least ₹1 lakh, or a bank deposit certificate for a similar amount made at least 3 months before applying

Fees and payment

Fees for Indian passport holders, as per the Embassy's fee notice:

Payment is by demand draft only; no cash or personal cheques. The draft must be printed by the bank, with the applicant's full name, pick-up slip number and phone number written on the back. For the Delhi Embassy, make it payable to "CHINESE EMBASSY, NEW DELHI". If you are travelling in 2027, check the current fees before applying.

  • Single entry: ₹2,900
  • Double entry: ₹4,400
  • 6-month multiple entry: ₹5,900
  • 12-month-plus multiple entry: ₹8,800
  • Group visa, per person: ₹1,800
  • Express processing (4 working days): ₹1,800 extra
  • Urgent processing (2 working days, where available): ₹2,700 extra

What to do

  • Check the latest notice on the Chinese Embassy website (in.china-embassy.gov.cn) before applying, as rules can change at short notice.
  • Get 48mm × 33mm photos and, for a tourist visa, your 6-month bank statements or deposit certificate ready before filling the online form.
  • Plan time for the in-person submission in New Delhi within 30 days of approval, and arrange a bank-printed demand draft for the fee.
