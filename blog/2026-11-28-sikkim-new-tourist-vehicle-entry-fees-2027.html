Sikkim to charge new vehicle entry fees at Nathula, Tsomgo from 2027

According to reports of a Sikkim Home Department notification, new per-vehicle entry fees for certain Reserve Forest areas start on 1 January 2027. If you are planning a New Year or 2027 Sikkim trip from Hyderabad, check the charges with your operator before you finalise your plan.

28 November 2026 · Travel advisory

What has been announced

Reports say the Sikkim Home Department issued Notification No. 127/Home/2026 on 23 November 2026, signed by Chief Secretary R. Telang. The revised guidelines take effect on 1 January 2027.

We have read news reports of the notification, not the notification itself. So please treat the figures below as reported, and confirm the final charges before you travel.

Where the fees apply

The fees apply to designated Reserve Forest areas in the Gangtok, Pakyong and Mangan divisions. They do not apply to every vehicle entering Sikkim.

In the Gangtok-Pakyong division, the routes covered are Nathula, Tsomgo (Changu) Lake, Baba Mandir, Dzuluk and Lingtam. In Mangan, the fees apply to areas beyond the Sankalang-Toong and Phedang check posts, which covers regulated routes in North Sikkim.

These are the places many Hyderabad families visit on day trips from Gangtok, usually by vehicle.

Reported charges by vehicle

The fee depends on seating capacity, including the driver, and on the division. Figures below are given as Gangtok-Pakyong / Mangan.

  • Four-wheeler, 5 seats, above 1,000 cc: ₹1,500 / ₹1,200
  • 6–10 seats: ₹2,500 / ₹1,500
  • Force or Tempo Traveller, 11–15 seats: ₹4,500 / ₹2,500
  • Bus, 16–20 seats: ₹6,500 / ₹3,500
  • Bus, 21–30 seats: ₹7,500 / ₹5,500
  • Bus, more than 30 seats: ₹10,000 / ₹7,500
  • Private motorcycles: ₹750, plus ₹350 for a pillion rider
  • Local Sikkim-registered taxi motorcycles: ₹350, plus ₹250 for a pillion rider

What it means for your trip

The reports do not say how the charge is collected or who pays it. They also do not say whether it applies per trip, per day or per entry. It is not clear whether it will be added to a hired vehicle's fare or paid separately.

Sikkimese residents with valid documents are reported to be exempt, subject to verification.

Rules like this can change, so check the current notification and final charges before you travel. Outlook Traveller also advises checking charges with operators before finalising 2027 itineraries.

What to do

  • If you are travelling on or after 1 January 2027, ask your tour operator or driver which fee applies to your vehicle and division.
  • Ask how the charge will be passed on to you, and whether it is per trip, per day or per entry.
  • Check the latest notification and final charges before you finalise your itinerary.
