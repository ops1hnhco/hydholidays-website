Hyderabad Airport Runway 09R Upgraded for Fog: What Flyers Should Know

GMR Hyderabad International Airport Ltd (GHIAL) says it has commissioned CAT III capability for Runway 09R, meant to help flights operate in low visibility during the fog season. For travellers from Hyderabad, it is a welcome step, but fog delays can still happen, so plan with buffer time.

11 November 2026 · Flights & airports

What GHIAL has announced

GHIAL has commissioned CAT III Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) capability for Runway 09R at Hyderabad International Airport, according to Telangana Today and UNI India, both published on 10 November 2026. The runway moves from CAT II to CAT III capability.

GHIAL says the upgrade lets aircraft operate in much lower visibility. It is meant to reduce weather-related diversions and delays, especially in the winter fog season. UNI adds that it should also help airlines keep operating in adverse weather.

What the upgrade includes

As reported, the work covers the following:

  • CAT III-compliant runway lighting
  • Advanced monitoring systems
  • Stand Manoeuvring Guidance Lights (SMGL) to help pilots
  • An India-developed PLC-SCADA-based system for central monitoring and control of the lighting from the Airport Predictive Operation Centre (APOC)

What it means for your trip

GHIAL CEO Kadhir Kadhiravan said: "For passengers, this means fewer delays, smoother journeys, and a more dependable travel experience." Delays and diversions are expected to fall, but no figures have been given.

Some points are not yet clear. The reports do not say from when the runway is in use. Whether a particular flight can land in very low visibility also depends on the airline, the aircraft and the crew being CAT III-certified, and we have not confirmed this for any airline. So we cannot say which airlines or flights will benefit.

This is GHIAL's own announcement, carried by two outlets. We have not seen a DGCA or AAI statement confirming it. Fog disruption can still happen this winter.

The upgrade matters most if you have a tight connection, a cruise departure, a visa start date or an event with a fixed date. In such cases, a little planning goes a long way.

What to do

  • Check your flight status with the airline before leaving for the airport.
  • Allow extra time for connections and for onward travel with fixed dates, such as cruises, events or visa validity.
  • Avoid very tight layovers on winter itineraries.
