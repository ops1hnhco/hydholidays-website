Banaras Biennale brings a month of culture to Varanasi ghats

The first CII Banaras Biennale runs in Varanasi from 27 November to 31 December 2026, with exhibitions and cultural programmes across the ghats, museums and universities. For Hyderabad travellers planning a Kashi trip this winter, it offers music, textile and craft experiences to add to the visit.

27 November 2026 · Destinations

What the Biennale is

The Banaras Biennale is a new event backed by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII). The first edition runs for 35 days, from 27 November to 31 December 2026. Its theme is "Kashi/light: The City as a Sensorium".

The programme is spread across the ghats, museums, universities and streets of Varanasi. It includes exhibitions, cultural programmes, curated walkthroughs, training workshops and textile factory tours.

The opening day, 27 November, begins with a pre-dawn visit to the Kashi Vishwanath temple. That day is by invitation. The public programme starts from 28 November.

Preview Week and the December programme

Preview Week runs from 27 November to 1 December. According to the official FAQ, it includes museum collection showcases, curated museum walks and a Film Festival. It also has a Food Festival and Handicraft Haat at Namo Ghat. These two are listed only for Preview Week, not for the whole month.

From 2 to 31 December, the exhibitions continue as per their individual listings. Textile exhibitions and a participatory artwork, "Ek Dhaaga, Ek Duniya", are part of the programme.

On the evening of 28 November, Namo Ghat will host live music. Deobrat Mishra and Trio Benares will perform, followed by "Three Generations of Sitar".

Venues and another festival

The main venues are listed in the official FAQ. Each venue has its own access arrangements.

Business Standard also reports that the Mahindra Kabira Festival, in its 10th edition, is due on the ghats from 4 to 6 December. It is expected to include folk music, boat rides, a food trail and heritage walks. We have this from one source, so please confirm it before planning around it.

  • Banaras Hindu University, including Bharat Kala Bhavan
  • Jnana Pravaha
  • Nagari Pracharini Sabha
  • Namo Ghat
  • Peshwa Haveli

Planning your visit

Many families already visit Kashi for darshan and the ghats. The Biennale gives those who travel in December something more to add, such as a museum walk, a textile exhibition or an evening of music.

Visitors can register through the official website. We could not confirm ticket details, and session timings may change. Check venue, timings, access rules and any registration needed for each exhibition on ciibanarasbiennale.in before you go.

What to do

  • Visit ciibanarasbiennale.in to check the venue, timings and registration needs for each exhibition you want to see.
  • Note that the Food Festival and Handicraft Haat at Namo Ghat are listed for 27 November to 1 December only.
  • Pick your travel dates around the events you want, and confirm the Kabira Festival dates if you plan to attend.
