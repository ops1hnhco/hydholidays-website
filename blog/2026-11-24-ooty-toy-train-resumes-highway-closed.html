Ooty toy train is running again, but the highway is still closed

Toy train services on the Mettupalayam hill route resumed on 19 Nov after a 26-day suspension caused by landslides, but the Ooty–Coimbatore national highway was still closed as of 23 Nov. Families from Hyderabad planning Ooty trips should check the current route before they travel.

24 November 2026 · Travel advisory

Toy train services have resumed

Heavy rain and landslides disrupted access to the Nilgiris in October. The Nilgiri Mountain Railway, popularly called the toy train, was suspended from 24 Oct to 18 Nov.

Toy train services on the Mettupalayam hill route resumed on Thursday, 19 Nov 2026, after 26 days. Reports say railway staff cleared boulders, soil and uprooted trees from over 50 spots on the track before services could restart.

Reports differ slightly on which stretch of the route has resumed, so please check train timings and ticket availability with Indian Railways before you plan around the ride.

Highway still closed as of 23 Nov

A major landslide hit the Burliar stretch of the Ooty–Coimbatore National Highway on the night of 23 Oct. The road was damaged at three places and traffic was stopped.

As of 20 Nov, temporary restoration work was nearing completion and awaited inspection by geology experts before traffic could resume. As of 23 Nov, the highway was still closed.

The Ooty–Kotagiri–Coimbatore road is the alternative route for now. We do not have details of its journey time or road condition, so please do not assume either.

The reopening date of the highway is not confirmed. It may reopen within days, so the situation can change quickly.

Tourists are returning to Ooty

With winter setting in and the toy train running again, Ooty saw a big rush of tourists over the weekend of 21–22 Nov.

If you are planning a December trip, keep your plans flexible. Hill roads can be affected when it rains, so it helps to confirm the route and your hotel details close to your travel date.

We can arrange hotels and transfers for Ooty. Because the highway is closed, road transfers currently run via Kotagiri. Ask us for the current route before you travel.

What to do

  • Check the current status of the Ooty–Coimbatore highway just before you leave, as it may reopen soon.
  • Confirm toy train timings and ticket availability with Indian Railways.
  • WhatsApp us on +91 70750 40406 to check the current route and arrange your hotel and transfers.
  • Keep some buffer time in your itinerary in case of delays on hill roads.
