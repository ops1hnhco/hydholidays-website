Gulmarg and Sonamarg get first snow of the season

Gulmarg and Sonamarg in Kashmir received the season's first snowfall on 12-13 November, drawing tourists while disrupting some mountain roads. Hyderabad families planning December or New Year trips can use this to set expectations for snow, crowds and road conditions.

23 November 2026 · Destinations

What happened in Gulmarg and Sonamarg

Gulmarg got its first snowfall of the season on 12-13 November, according to Greater Kashmir. Kongdori, which is reached by the Gulmarg Gondola, received a good amount of snow. A local hotelier said tourist flow had gone up sharply over the previous two days.

WION reported hundreds of tourists at the Gondola as snow covered the upper reaches of the Apharwat range. Some higher-altitude areas recorded 2-6 inches. Rising Kashmir said visitors from different parts of the country headed to the upper Gondola phases and to Sonamarg.

Roads were disrupted for a few days

The snow affected some mountain roads. Kashmir Observer reported that Sonamarg got 2-3 inches and Zojila Pass got 5-6 inches. The Mughal Road (Shopian-Poonch/Rajouri) was closed for a second day. BRO began clearing the snow, and reopening depended on the weather.

Authorities advised caution on slippery roads. Travellers and tourists were asked to plan journeys carefully and follow traffic advisories.

  • Sonamarg: 2-3 inches
  • Zojila Pass: 5-6 inches
  • Mughal Road: closed for a second day, reopening weather-dependent

Weather outlook and crowds

IMD forecast generally dry weather from 15 to 24 November, with partly cloudy skies on 20-21 November. Rising Kashmir said the Met Department expected a prolonged dry spell from 15 November. Greater Kashmir reported that a feeble western disturbance on 14 November brought light rain and snow to higher reaches, with improvement expected from 15 November.

These forecasts were made around 13-14 November, and we have no later reports, so please do not treat them as current conditions. The first-snow reports describe light to moderate snow, not full winter cover. Snow depth changes by day and location, and no date can be promised.

The Tourism Department expects arrivals to increase in the coming days, and Gulmarg traditionally gets more visitors as winter sets in. For December or New Year trips, expect busier days around the Gondola. Gondola timings, ticket availability and prices were not in the reports, so check these with the operator.

What to do

  • Check the IMD/Met Srinagar forecast and local traffic advisories just before you travel.
  • Keep your plans flexible, as roads like the Mughal Road and Zojila can close at short notice.
  • Confirm Gondola timings and ticket availability with the operator.
