Rann Utsav 2026-27 is on in Kutch till 7 March 2027

The 2026-27 Rann Utsav season opened on 1 November 2026 at Dhordo in Kutch and runs to 7 March 2027. If you are planning a December–January break, a Christmas trip or a Sankranti holiday, there is still plenty of time to build a Kutch trip around it.

17 November 2026 · Destinations

Season dates and the moon calendar

The festival is held at Dhordo, Kutch, from 1 November 2026 to 7 March 2027. The official Rann Utsav site describes itself as the official tented accommodation experience, appointed by Gujarat Tourism.

The White Rann looks different depending on the moon, so many travellers pick their dates around it. One source, Outlook Traveller, lists these periods for the season:

  • Full-moon periods: 23–25 Nov, 22–24 Dec, 21–23 Jan and 19–21 Feb
  • Dark-moon dates, better for stargazing, include 8 Dec, 7 Jan and 6 Feb

Where you stay and what you can do

Tent City stays range from Swiss cottages to premium tented suites. The Darbari and Rajwadi Suites are at the top end. Tariffs, inclusions and availability change by date, so confirm them with the official operator before you pay.

Activities include folk performances and a craft haat, or market, with Ajrakh, embroidery, Bandhani, mirror work and Lippan art. The White Rann at sunrise and sunset is the main attraction. PuneNow also lists camel safaris and jeep tours, and says meals are strictly vegetarian with Jain options.

Dhordo was named one of the UN World Tourism Organization's Best Tourism Villages in 2023.

Sights nearby and the weather

Many visitors add a few days of sightseeing around the festival. Nearby places mentioned include:

  • Dholavira, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Aina Mahal in Bhuj
  • Kala Dungar
  • Mandvi Beach
  • Vijay Vilas Palace

Weather and planning your dates

Typical temperatures are 20–28°C in November and 12–25°C in December and January. These are usual ranges, not forecasts, so pack for cool evenings and check the weather closer to your dates.

The moon dates above come from a single source. Check them again before you fix your travel dates. We can plan a Kutch or Gujarat itinerary with hotel and tent stays to suit the dates you choose.

What to do

  • Decide whether you want a full-moon night or a dark-moon night for stargazing, then shortlist dates.
