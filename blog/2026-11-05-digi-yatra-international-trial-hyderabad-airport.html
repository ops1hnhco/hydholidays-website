Digi Yatra trial for international flights at Hyderabad airport

The Civil Aviation Ministry said on 1 November 2026 that Digi Yatra trials for international passengers have started at seven airports, including Hyderabad. Only Indian e-passport holders on select Air India and IndiGo flights can use it, so check your eligibility before you fly.

5 November 2026 · Flights & airports

What has started

The Civil Aviation Ministry said on Sunday, 1 November 2026, that Digi Yatra trials for international passengers have begun at seven airports. Digi Yatra is the facial-recognition system used for passenger checks at airports.

The seven airports are Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Cochin, Mumbai, Chennai and Amritsar. As reported by PTI, the trial period runs from November to December 2026. Feedback from it will be used to plan a wider rollout, which is expected in 2027. The 2027 date is not confirmed.

Who can use it

The trial is open only to Indian nationals holding e-passports. It covers select international flights operated by Air India and IndiGo.

Eligible passengers can use the facility at terminal entry points, Security Hold Areas and boarding gates, "wherever the required infrastructure is available". We have not confirmed which Hyderabad flights, terminals or touchpoints are included.

It does not replace other checks

The ministry says Digi Yatra is meant to complement existing travel processes, not replace them. You must still carry your travel documents. Airline document checks, immigration and other formalities still apply.

How to register

An eligible passenger creates an e-passport-based Verifiable Credential in the Digi Yatra mobile app. You need a phone with NFC to do this. The steps are:

  • Scan the machine-readable zone of your passport.
  • Read the passport chip using your phone's NFC.
  • Take a selfie and verify the details.

Digi Yatra so far

The credential is stored in the Digi Yatra wallet on your phone. Digi Yatra has over 26.5 million users and has enabled more than 110 million journeys across 39 domestic airports. A further 25 domestic airports are planned by 2027.

For Hyderabad travellers who fly abroad on Air India or IndiGo, the trial could mean shorter queues at some points. The rules are narrow and the trial is short, so do not rely on it for your next trip.

What to do

  • Check that your passport has the chip symbol on the cover. Without it, you cannot use the facility.
  • Ask Air India or IndiGo, or check the Digi Yatra app, whether your flight and Hyderabad airport touchpoints are covered.
  • Carry your passport and other travel documents as usual, and plan for normal airline checks and immigration.
  • Message us on WhatsApp for help with flights, hotels and your trip plan.
