Sikkim 5N/6D package: Gangtok and Darjeeling at ₹23,200

Hyderabad Holidays Co. is offering a Sikkim 5N/6D package covering Gangtok and Darjeeling at ₹23,200 per person on double sharing, subject to availability. Travellers from Hyderabad can WhatsApp us to book before 05 Jan 2027.

21 November 2026 · Offers

The trip

We have a Sikkim package that you can book with us now. It is a 5 nights, 6 days trip covering Gangtok and Darjeeling, with stays in 3* Budget hotels: Lotus Inn Samphel, White Yak or Yuma.

The price is ₹23,200 per person on double sharing. This is our price and it is subject to availability. Prices and availability can change until the booking is confirmed, so it is better to enquire early.

Sightseeing is done in a private car, so your group travels together at its own pace.

What is included

Your package covers the main arrangements for the trip:

  • Pickup & drop NJP & Bagdogra Airport
  • Breakfast and dinner at hotel
  • Hotel accommodation on double sharing
  • Sightseeing in private car (2-3 pax WagonR non-AC, 4-6 pax Innova/Xylo non-AC)

What is not included

Please keep some budget aside for costs that are not part of the package. These include:

  • Personal expenses such as river rafting, rock climbing, paragliding and the toy train joyride
  • Entry fee, camera fee, parking fee and guide charges
  • Expenses due to natural calamity, accident or strikes

Dates and who it suits

Travel dates are 20 Dec to 05 Jan 2027. The book-by date is 05 Jan 2027.

The package suits couples, families and small groups from Hyderabad who want to see Gangtok and Darjeeling with hotel stay, meals and a private car sorted. A group of 2-3 travellers gets a WagonR, and a group of 4-6 gets an Innova or Xylo, both non-AC.

If you are unsure about anything that is or is not covered, ask us before you confirm. We will go through the details with you.

What to do

  • WhatsApp us on +91 70750 40406 with your preferred dates and the number of travellers.
  • Ask us to check availability for your dates and which of the three hotels is open.
  • Keep extra money for activities, fees and guide charges that are not included.
  • Confirm your booking early, and no later than 05 Jan 2027.
