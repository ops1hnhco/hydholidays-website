Jatayu Earth's Centre in Kollam sees crowds rise after Ramayana film

Jatayu Earth's Centre in Chadayamangalam, Kollam, is seeing bigger crowds after the release of "Ramayana: Part 1", along with the Diwali rush. Hyderabad families adding it to a Kerala trip should plan for crowds, check tickets and timings, and go early on a weekday.

19 November 2026 · Destinations

Why the crowds are rising

Jatayu Earth's Centre, at Chadayamangalam in Kollam district, has become busier since "Ramayana: Part 1" was released on 5 November. Onmanorama reports that visitor numbers began rising after the film came out.

The crowd figures differ between sources. Onmanorama reports daily footfall of up to 7,000 or more, with most visitors from other states. The centre's CEO, Deepak Sunil, told The Federal that it gets about 2,500 to 3,000 visitors on normal days and about 5,000 on weekends. He also said the centre can handle over 8,000 a day.

The CEO says only some of the crowd is due to the film. The Diwali rush is also a reason. Onmanorama expects numbers to rise further as the Sabarimala pilgrimage season begins.

What you can see and what it costs

The centre is open daily from 10 am to 5 pm. It is about 38 km from Kollam city and 50 km from Thiruvananthapuram. October to March is the best season.

Attractions reported by Onmanorama include a multi-level audio-visual museum and a 6D theatre inside the sculpture, and the Kodandarama temple on top of the rock. There are also adventure activities such as ziplining, rock climbing, rappelling and paintball.

Ticket rates reported by Onmanorama are ranges, not an official price list:

  • Standard entry to view the main sculpture: ₹250 to ₹400
  • Swiss cable car: ₹550 to ₹650 per person
  • Walkway tickets for those trekking up: ₹300 to ₹350
  • Adventure park: separate ticket for each activity

Rules and planning tips

The Federal reports that outside food, plastic and drones are not allowed on the hills. Items held at the gate are returned when you leave. Drinking water is free inside. Most people spend one to two hours.

There is no time limit on visits yet, but the CEO says one may be brought in if crowds grow.

A museum and a multi-dimensional theatre re-creating the Jatayu–Ravana fight are planned, and work has begun. Accommodation is also mentioned for the future. These are only plans with no opening date, so do not count on them for now.

Hyderabad families often combine Kerala with Munnar, Alappuzha and Varkala. Jatayu Earth's Centre can fit into such a trip. The CEO says visitors from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana come throughout the year.

What to do

  • Go on a weekday and reach early, as weekends and festival periods are busiest.
  • Confirm current ticket prices and timings with the centre before you travel, as the rates here are reported ranges.
  • Leave outside food, plastic and drones behind, and keep 1 to 2 hours for the visit.
