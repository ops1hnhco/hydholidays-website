New MHA ID rules for Indians travelling to Nepal and Bhutan

The Home Ministry's amended exemption order, effective 5 November 2026, requires Indians travelling to or from Nepal or Bhutan to show a listed identity document, and does not exempt passport rules for onward trips to other countries. Travellers from Hyderabad should carry a passport, or an original Voter ID where accepted, and not rely on Aadhaar, PAN or a driving licence.

7 November 2026 · Travel advisory

What the new order says

The Immigration and Foreigners (Exemption) (Amendment) Order, 2026 was issued under Section 33 of the Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025. It came into effect on 5 November 2026 after publication in the Official Gazette. It is notified as S.O. 6027(E) on 4 November and amends the original exemption order notified in September 2025.

Indians travelling to or from Nepal or Bhutan, by land or air, must show at least one identity document listed in the government's Visa Manual to an immigration officer. They must also give any further information the officer asks for.

The notification does not reproduce the full list of acceptable IDs. That list is in the Visa Manual, and we have not been able to confirm its full contents.

Going on to a third country

The exemption does not apply to Indians travelling to or returning from countries other than Nepal or Bhutan through the India–Nepal or India–Bhutan borders.

Anyone planning such a trip must hold a valid passport and enter or leave India only through designated immigration posts.

Outlook Traveller also reports that Aadhaar, PAN and driving licences are not valid substitutes for air travel to Nepal.   • A valid Indian passport
  • An original photo Voter ID issued by the Election Commission. Printed or downloaded copies are not accepted.

A note on these details

The ID details above come from media reports of the embassy and Bhutan's guidance, not from the official pages themselves. Rules can change, so confirm current requirements before you travel. The safest choice is to carry your passport.

What to do

  • Carry a valid passport for Nepal or Bhutan. If you rely on a Voter ID, carry the original photo card, not a copy.
  • Do not depend on Aadhaar, PAN or a driving licence as your only ID for these trips.
  • If you plan to go onward from Nepal or Bhutan to another country, make sure your passport is valid and use only designated immigration posts.
  • Check the latest requirements with your airline, the Indian Embassy in Kathmandu or Bhutan's Department of Immigration before travelling.
