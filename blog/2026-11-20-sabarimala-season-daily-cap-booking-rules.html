Sabarimala season begins: 80,000 daily cap and booking rules

The Sabarimala Mandala-Makaravilakku season has begun, with daily darshan capped at 80,000 and entry managed through a virtual queue and spot-booking centres. Pilgrims from Hyderabad, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh should book on the official portal and check the rules before they leave.

20 November 2026 · Travel advisory

The season has started

The Sabarimala temple opened on 16 November 2026 to begin the Mandala-Makaravilakku season. Regular poojas and darshan started on 17 November, with new chief priests opening the shrine. Makaravilakku falls on 14 January 2027.

Many Ayyappa devotees from Telugu families make this pilgrimage every year. Early reports spoke of a heavy rush, so planning ahead matters more than ever.

How entry is managed

Daily darshan is limited to 80,000 pilgrims. As per the allocation reported by DT Next, 66,000 slots go through the online virtual queue and 14,000 through spot booking. Exact numbers on any given day may differ.

Free darshan slots are booked on the virtual queue portal, https://sabarimalaonline.org. Bookings for the season opened on 29 October at 10 am. Children under five do not need virtual queue passes.

  • Spot-booking centres: Nilakkal, Erumeli and Vandiperiyar Sathram.
  • Daily spot-booking allocation: 10,000 at Nilakkal, and 2,000 each at Erumeli and Vandiperiyar Sathram.
  • There is no spot booking at Pamba.
  • Without a virtual queue booking, a spot pass is available only subject to availability. Please do not assume you will get one.

Documents, timings and facilities

Carry your original Aadhaar or a photocopy, along with your virtual queue confirmation. The QR code on the pass is scanned and identity is checked before entry.

During the season the temple is open 18 hours a day, from 3 am to 1 pm and from 3 pm to 11 pm. Neyyabhishekam is held from 3.30 am to 11 am.

Medical facilities run round the clock at Sannidhanam, Pamba and Nilakkal. A 24-hour health control room works at Pamba. The emergency number given is 0473 5203232.

Crowd control and safety

Onmanorama reports AI-enabled cameras, waiting tents for pilgrims and tighter transport regulation. Police aim to move about 75 devotees a minute through the 18 holy steps.

If you travel by train, do not light camphor, incense or lamps inside coaches or at stations. Flammable or explosive items are prohibited under railway safety rules.

The Pamba trek, the Erumeli forest route and the Vandiperiyar-Sathram route have different requirements, so check the rules for your route. Elderly pilgrims and people with heart or breathing problems should see a doctor before the uphill trek.

What to do

  • Book your free darshan slot only on the official portal, https://sabarimalaonline.org, and keep the confirmation with your original Aadhaar or a photocopy.
  • Check https://sabarimalaonline.org and https://sabarimala.kerala.gov.in for the latest notices on limits, routes and crowd arrangements before you leave.
  • Confirm the rules for your chosen route and consult a doctor first if you have heart or breathing problems or are elderly.
