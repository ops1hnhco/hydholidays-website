Saudi flight suspensions extended: what Hyderabad flyers should do

IndiGo extended its suspension of flights to Saudi Arabia on 10 November, and other Indian airlines have also suspended Saudi routes. Hyderabad travellers with Umrah or Gulf bookings in the coming weeks should check their airline's latest dates and refund options.

13 November 2026 · Flights & airports

IndiGo's new dates

On 10 November, IndiGo extended its suspension of flights to Saudi Arabia again. The airline says it is notifying affected passengers using the contact details given at the time of booking. It is offering re-accommodation, refunds or alternative arrangements.

The suspensions now run as follows:

  • Jeddah and Madinah: suspended until 21 November 2026 inclusive
  • Riyadh and Dammam: suspended until 30 November 2026 inclusive

Why, and what other airlines are doing

Business Standard links the extension to renewed hostilities between Saudi Arabia and Yemen's Houthi rebels. It reports that on 8 November Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Energy confirmed an attempted attack on a Saudi Aramco administrative building in Riyadh, with minor damage and no injuries. The Houthis have claimed other attacks, including on King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, but those are their own claims. Gulf News also reports that European aviation authorities have kept conflict-zone warnings for the Gulf and nearby airspace in place until 16 November.

These are airline suspensions and warnings. The reports do not say that Saudi airspace or airports are closed.

Other Indian airlines, as per the latest reports we read:

  • Akasa Air: Jeddah suspended until 18 November, Riyadh until 30 November
  • Air India and Air India Express: Jeddah, Riyadh and Dammam suspended until 14 November inclusive, as per their 30 October advisory. We found no later announcement from either airline, so please check with them directly.

Refunds and rebooking

Terms differ by airline and by the date of booking. Air India's 30 October advisory gives passengers with confirmed bookings issued on or before 30 October, for travel up to 14 November, one free rebooking for travel up to 29 November, subject to availability. They can ask for a full refund instead. Air India can be reached on +91 11 69329333 and +91 11 69329999, and Air India Express on +91 124 6935600.

Akasa Air offers a full refund to the original payment method within seven days, or free rescheduling. If you booked through a travel agent, the request should be handled through the agent.

Alternatives and help for pilgrims in Saudi Arabia

According to the Consulate General of India in Jeddah, Saudi carriers were still flying between Saudi Arabia and India. Saudi carrier flynas announced extra flights on select dates from Dammam to Hyderabad and from Riyadh to Hyderabad, among other Indian cities. These details date from 1 November, so please confirm they are still current.

Umrah pilgrims facing difficulties during an extended stay can call the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah's Pilgrim Care Center on 1966 from within Saudi Arabia, and should note the case reference number. The Consulate runs 24x7 control rooms in Jeddah and Makkah, and says it is in touch with Indian airlines about refund difficulties.

What to do

  • Check your airline's website or app for the latest dates before you leave for the airport, as they may change again.
  • Ask your airline, or your travel agent if you booked through one, about refund or rebooking options before you decide.
  • If you plan to use an alternative carrier such as flynas, confirm the flight and date first.
  • If you are already in Saudi Arabia and face problems, call 1966 and note your case reference number.
