Thailand visa for Indians

Indian passport holders can visit Thailand for tourism without a visa for up to 30 days. The 60-day exemption ended on 15 September 2026.

Visa typeVisa exemption
Stay30 days (extendable 30)
Government feeFree
Arrival cardTDAC, within 72 hours
Good to know: If you entered before 15 September 2026, the stay stamped in your passport still applies. For business trips or stays over 30 days, get a visa before you fly; you cannot convert the exemption after landing.

Processing time: No processing for the visa exemption. Apply for an e-visa at least 2–3 weeks before travel.

Documents checklist

  • Passport valid at least 6 months with a blank page
  • Confirmed return or onward ticket within 30 days
  • Hotel booking
  • Proof of funds; carry THB 20,000 per person in cash or equivalent
  • TDAC confirmation (Thailand Digital Arrival Card)

How we apply for you

  2. We confirm the right visa and send a personalised checklist.

Our service fee is listed on the Pricing page. Government fees are charged at actuals and are not refundable if a visa is refused.

Before you fly

  • The TDAC is free and must be filed within 72 hours before arrival at tdac.immigration.go.th. Paid lookalike sites exist.
  • Immigration officers can check funds and refuse entry without them.
  • Do not accept job offers in Thailand; there are active scam warnings for Indian travellers.
