Kerala 6N/7D tour package from Hyderabad: Munnar, Thekkady, Alleppey and Kovalam

The classic Kerala loop with two extra nights by the sea: tea hills in Munnar, the Periyar forests at Thekkady, a houseboat night in Alleppey, then the beaches of Kovalam near Thiruvananthapuram.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrive Kochi, drive to Munnar past waterfalls and spice gardens
  2. Day 2Munnar: Eravikulam National Park, tea museum, Mattupetty Dam and Echo Point
  3. Day 3Drive to Thekkady: spice plantation walk, optional Periyar lake boat ride
  4. Day 4Drive to Alleppey, board a private houseboat and cruise the backwaters
  5. Day 5Drive to Kovalam, evening at Lighthouse beach
  6. Day 6Kovalam and Thiruvananthapuram: Padmanabhaswamy temple, Napier Museum, beach time
  7. Day 7Drop at Thiruvananthapuram airport or railway station

What's included

Included

  • 2 nights in Munnar, 1 in Thekkady and 2 in Kovalam in 3-star hotels, with daily breakfast
  • 1 night on a private houseboat in Alleppey with all meals on board
  • Private car with driver for all transfers and sightseeing
  • Pickup at Kochi, drop at Thiruvananthapuram airport or railway station

Not included

  • Entry tickets, Periyar boat ride, Kathakali and other optional shows
  • Temple dress code items for the Padmanabhaswamy temple
  • Lunch, dinner and personal expenses
  • Optional activities, tips and porterage
  • GST as applicable

Good to know

  • Best time to visit: September to March. The June–August monsoon is quieter and greener, good for Ayurveda stays.
