Goa 6N/7D tour package from Hyderabad: 3 nights North, 3 nights South

A relaxed week split between two hotels: three nights in North Goa for the forts and busy beaches, then three in South Goa near Colva for quiet sands, Cabo de Rama and Palolem.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrive in Goa, check in near Calangute, evening at Calangute and Baga beaches
  2. Day 2North Goa: Fort Aguada, Sinquerim, Anjuna and Vagator beaches, Chapora Fort
  3. Day 3Old Goa and Panjim: Basilica of Bom Jesus, Se Cathedral, Mangueshi temple, Dona Paula; optional evening Mandovi river cruise
  4. Day 4Move to a South Goa hotel near Colva, afternoon at Colva and Benaulim beaches
  5. Day 5South Goa: Cabo de Rama fort, Palolem beach, optional boat trip to Butterfly beach
  6. Day 6Optional day trip to Dudhsagar falls and a spice plantation, or a free beach day
  7. Day 7Check out and drop at the airport or railway station

What's included

Included

  • 3 nights in North Goa and 3 nights in South Goa in 3-star hotels, with daily breakfast
  • Airport or railway station pickup and drop, and the transfer between hotels
  • North Goa, Old Goa and South Goa sightseeing by AC vehicle

Not included

  • Entry tickets, water sports and the Mandovi river cruise
  • Dudhsagar falls trip (optional, priced separately)
  • Lunch, dinner and personal expenses
  • Optional activities, tips and porterage
  • GST as applicable

Good to know

  • Best time to visit: November to February. Monsoon (June–September) is lush but many beach shacks close.
  • Customise it: add nights, upgrade hotels or make it a private trip for your family or group.
